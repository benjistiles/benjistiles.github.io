About

Bassist, multi-instrumentalist, and educator.

Benji Stiles is a musician based in Michigan. He holds a Bachelor of Music in Commercial Music from Anderson University, where he studied electric and upright bass, drum set and percussion, songwriting, composition, and audio engineering. He is currently pursuing a Master of Music in Jazz Studies at Michigan State University, studying with bassist Rodney Whitaker.

His work includes jazz and commercial performance, studio sessions, arranging, private instruction, and audio engineering. Before moving to Michigan, he served as a studio technician at Anderson University and performed with the Anderson Symphony Orchestra, AUthenticity, jazz ensembles, and other collaborative groups.
