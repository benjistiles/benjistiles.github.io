About

Musician, educator,
and collaborator.

Benji Stiles is a musician based in Michigan, with a background in commercial music, jazz, studio work, and performance leadership.

He holds a Bachelor of Music in Commercial Music from Anderson University, where he studied electric and upright bass, drum set and percussion, songwriting, composition, and audio engineering. He is currently pursuing a Master of Music in Jazz Studies at Michigan State University, studying with bassist Rodney Whitaker.

His work spans live performance, studio sessions, arranging, private instruction, and audio engineering—always with an emphasis on musical clarity, curiosity, and generous collaboration.
